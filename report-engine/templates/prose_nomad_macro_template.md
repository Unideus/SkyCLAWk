5. The Epochal Shift: From Material Earth to Networked Air
Beyond the short-term generational rhythm of the Saeculum lies a massive civilizational engine: the Great Elemental Cycles. For roughly two centuries at a time, the continuous alignments of Jupiter and Saturn occur exclusively within signs of a single element. Your life spans one of the rarest historical inflection points in human history—the literal death of the material Earth Era and the birth of the networked Air Era.

From the early 19th century until 2020, humanity operated entirely under the Earth Era. This long epoch dictated the rules of global civilization: it was the age of coal, steel, transcontinental railroads, heavy industry, physical suburban expansion, and centralized state power. The hard reset arrived in 2020 when Saturn and Jupiter met in Aquarius, permanently locking the civilizational clock into the Air Era until the year 2219.

That era shattered the rigid, localized feudal structures of Europe, replacing them with the rise of the first universities, the creation of the Magna Carta, the sweeping engineering of Gothic architecture, and a borderless rediscovery of classical knowledge.
