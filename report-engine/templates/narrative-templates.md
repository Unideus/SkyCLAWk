## Big Picture Executive Summary

### Template

> You were born in **[Year]** during the **[Saeculum Name]** saeculum, a **[Turning]** turning marked by the **[Conjunction Sign/Element]** conjunction of **[Year]**. Your generation is the **[Archetype]** archetype — **[one-line archetype description]**. You entered life as the **[Element]** era was **[era-status: ending / underway / shifting]**, and the current shift into the Air era reshapes the stage on which your personal chart operates. This report maps your inner planets against that macro-timeline so you can see where your individual pattern intersects the larger weather.

### Archetype Descriptions

- **Prophet**: raised in crisis, shaped by ideals, tasked with vision in later life
- **Nomad**: raised in high, shaped by independence, tasked with survival craft
- **Hero**: raised in awakening, shaped by collective action, tasked with building
- **Artist**: raised in unraveling, shaped by sensitivity, tasked with integration

## Timeline Screw Connection Paragraph

### Template

> Your birth chart is the **top view** looking straight down the coaxial wire of time. The Timeline Screw is the **side view**: the same axis seen from the edge, where history spirals downward like threads on a bolt. Your generation sits at one turn of that screw. Your personal planets — Sun, Moon, Mercury, Venus, Mars — are local eddies on that larger thread. The fixed stars and precessional layers turn far more slowly; your daily life turns fast. All four perspectives look down the same wire. This report shows the top-down view; the desktop engine lets you spin the screw and see how your pattern drops through the seven layers.

### Short Version (for compact reports)

> Your chart is one cross-section of the Timeline Screw: the top-down view of a much larger spiral. Your generation occupies one full turn of that screw. Your personal planets are local currents on that thread.

## Usage in PDF Generator

For each report:
1. Look up birth year in `SAECULUM` table
2. Determine element era status
3. Fill the Big Picture template
4. Append either full or short Timeline Screw paragraph
5. Place both early in the report, before or after the Saeculum section
