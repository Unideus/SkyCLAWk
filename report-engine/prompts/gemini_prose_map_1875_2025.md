- **Saturn-Jupiter:** The 20-year saeculum pulse: generations, turnings, and the long elemental rhythm of Fire → Earth → Air → Water.
- **Saturn-Uranus:** The ~45-year rhythm of institutional breakthrough and reform: old structures crack, new systems are tested.
- **Saturn-Neptune:** The ~36-year cycle where reality meets illusion: ideology, propaganda, spiritual fashion, and the hard limits of dreams.
- **Saturn-Pluto:** The ~33-38-year cycle of structural death and rebirth: power, debt, taboo, and the demolition of old orders.
- **Uranus-Neptune:** The ~172-year cycle of mass consciousness and spiritual awakening: technology and myth merge or collide.
- **Uranus-Pluto:** The ~115-year cycle of revolutionary upheaval: liberation movements, underground forces, and the transformation of collective power.
- **Neptune-Pluto:** The ~492-year deepest cycle: empire, myth, underground transformation, and the slow reshaping of collective imagination.
