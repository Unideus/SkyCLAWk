# The Coaxial Circuit of Time — Final

## The Core Image

Time is a **coaxial circuit**: a central axis and an outer axis moving together.

- **Central axis** = the **Spirit World Axis** — ENP, Draco, electricity, counterspace. The pattern that does not move in space but carries the signal.
- **Outer axis** = the **Material World Axis** — NCP, magnetism, space. The measurable, rotating world.
- **Current flowing between them** = history.

This circuit runs whether you view it from above or from the side.

## Top View: Everything Is a Circle

Look straight down the central axis. You see **nested circles**:

- The center is the North Pole of the material axis.
- Around it spins the daily circle (houses).
- Around that, the annual circle (zodiac).
- Around that, the lunar circle.
- Around that, the saeculum circle.
- Around that, the precessional circle.
- Around that, the Yuga circle.

Every layer is a **circle** because every cycle is a complete rotation around the same central axis. Your birth chart is the snapshot of all these circles aligned at one moment.

## Side View: Everything Is a Sine Wave

Now look at the same circuit from the side. The circles become **sine waves**.

- The daily cycle becomes a fast oscillation.
- The annual cycle becomes a slower wave.
- The lunar cycle weaves between them.
- The saeculum becomes a long wave, one crest per generation.
- The elemental cycle modulates the amplitude over centuries.
- The precessional and Yuga cycles become very slow, deep waves.

Each cycle has its own wavelength, its own frequency, its own amplitude. They are not threads of one screw. They are **independent waves** moving along the same wire. Where they intersect, history happens.

## History and the Native

Your birth moment is a **static snapshot** taken while everything is moving.

- The camera freezes all the waves at one instant.
- The result is the birth chart — a flat circle made of many moving circles.
- But the waves never stopped. They keep moving.

So the native is not a fixed thing. The chart is the **starting waveform** — the exact phase of every cycle at the moment you were born. Your life is the continuation of that waveform through time, interacting with all the other continuing waves.

Transits are simply the current phase of the moving waves crossing the phase of your frozen birth-wave. When two waves meet at the same phase, something resonates.
